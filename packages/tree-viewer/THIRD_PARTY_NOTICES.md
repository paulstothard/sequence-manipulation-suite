## Biological example data

The Bcl-2 example in `examples/data/bcl2-vertebrates.*` is an intact clade
from Biopython's `Tests/PhyloXML/bcl_2.xml` at commit
`dc262b5c437e07a8cc1cfb8a734c0d84a4434b23`. The unmodified original and
full Biopython license are included in `examples/data/bcl2-source/`.
Species display names and group annotations were added by SMS3.
